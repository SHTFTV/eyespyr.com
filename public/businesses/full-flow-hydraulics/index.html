Services to discuss

Start with the work in front of you.

Hydraulic work

Describe the affected function, visible leak or change in performance. Confirm assessment, parts and repair scope for the particular machine directly with the shop.

Tractor & equipment repairs

Have the make, model, serial number and recent service history ready. Ask which machines the shop supports and how assessment and approval are handled.

Fabrication

Explain what the part or assembly needs to do. Supply useful photographs and dimensions; material, design and fit need agreement before work starts.

Snowplow installation & repairs

Discuss the vehicle, plow model, mounting arrangement and intended winter use. Compatibility, availability and scheduling are confirmed for the job.

Make the first call a useful one.

Bring the equipment details, symptoms, current location and timing you need. Ask about assessment charges, authorisation for additional work, transport and parts sourcing. Mobile service, emergency availability and completion dates are not assumed.
